Book 1 · Ages 9–13

Léo and the Order of the Golden Ladle

Léo can taste secrets hiding in soup. At Le Grand Panier, the chefs aren’t just cooking — they’re guarding magical recipes. Someone stole the Order’s most precious book. With sharp-tongued Colette, he races across rooftops, markets, and underground passages before the wrong people rewrite the world… one bite at a time.

The Venetian Gambit

A poison with no name. A city of masks. Venice calls when a politician loses his will — poisoned by a flavor that feels like nothing. Léo and Colette hunt through fog and canals… and discover the enemy’s next weapon is aimed straight at their own kitchen.

Inside the Order

Think candlelit kitchens and gilt-edged secrets — not parchment cosplay. The Order of the Golden Ladle guards recipes that hold memories, while conspiracies simmer under Paris nights. Exciting middle-grade intrigue: stolen secrets, hidden dangers, found-family apprentices — culinary mystery with a dash of light historical espionage. No gore. Plenty of wonder.
